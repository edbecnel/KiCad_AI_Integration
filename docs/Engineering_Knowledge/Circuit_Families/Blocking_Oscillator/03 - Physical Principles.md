# Purpose

Ground observed behavior in governing physical laws without conflating hypothesis with measurement.

# Governing principles

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Faraday's law | Changing flux in the core induces voltage on all windings |
| Ampère's law | Primary current establishes magnetizing flux while the transistor conducts |
| Conservation of energy | Stored magnetic energy plus losses account for supply energy per cycle |
| Semiconductor physics | BJT/MOSFET saturation, storage time, and breakdown limits define switching |
| Magnetic core behavior | B-H curve, saturation, and permeability set flux swing and losses |

# Family-specific cautions

Claims about anomalous energy behavior require **instrumented measurements** (supply current, primary/secondary waveforms, temperature). The KB supports reasoning transparency: label conjecture as conjecture until confirmed.
